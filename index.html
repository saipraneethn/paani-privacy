Paani: Save Water — Privacy Policy
Effective November 8, 2026

Paani: Save Water (com.paani.savewater) is an offline game for children and adults. The app does not collect or share personal data with the developer or third parties. It has no accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

The app saves village progress and your vibration preference in app storage on your device. Android may back up and restore this data according to your device settings. The developer does not receive or access these backups.

The app uses vibration for optional gameplay feedback. You can turn vibration off in the game. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or advertising identifier.

You can reset village progress using Start a new village after a season. To remove all locally saved settings and progress, clear the app storage in Android Settings. Android backups are controlled through your device settings.

Children can play without providing personal information. If you email support, the email provider processes your message and we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information or children's personal information.
